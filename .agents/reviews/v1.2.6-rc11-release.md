## Post-publication blocker

[The P1 review thread](https://github.com/vahid162/pinova/pull/30#discussion_r4057293767) remains open, unresolved, current, and attached to RC11's `src/Helper.php` success branch. WordPress can return `true` from `wp_safe_redirect()` even when output has already caused PHP to commit headers. RC11 exits immediately on that boolean, so it can still omit a usable `Location` header and bypass the nonblank fallback and `auth.redirect_failed` record.

The existing integration test forces `wp_safe_redirect()` to return `false`, and the browser test exercises the normal headers-available path. Both are valid but neither simulates a redirect reporting success as header state becomes unavailable. Therefore the green CI and release-integrity evidence prove exactly what they claim, but they do not disprove this product defect.

RC11 is immutable, so its tag, Release, notes, and assets must not be changed or deleted. The bounded remedy is a new reviewed candidate that checks header state before redirect dispatch and again before taking the success exit, adds a regression for the success-with-committed-headers case, and repeats the complete PR/main/publish/release chain.

## Documentation follow-up gate

The documentation-only evidence PR passed its exact-head Quality run, but the required merged-main [Quality run 35642055080](https://github.com/vahid162/pinova/actions/runs/35642055080) finished 15/16 after the real-browser pending-request case failed to restore focus. Its retained artifact is `account-ui-browser-35642055080-1` (ID `10658318437`, digest `sha256:3148b7bceb4ba897daedd2089b2138adb69432ac58ac124b2db6e3da90b09436`). The trace shows the dialog closed and background inertness was removed, while focus remained inactive on the live `.showlogin` link.

The failure is product-relevant rather than a reason to weaken or retry the assertion. WooCommerce may replace the fragment containing the captured opener while a request is pending; RC11 then calls `focus()` on the detached original node. RC12 must resolve the equivalent connected opener after fragment replacement, retain the lifecycle-token guard against stale callbacks, and deterministically replace the opener in browser coverage.
