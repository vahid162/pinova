# Pinova Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Participation must remain free from harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while evaluating evidence critically.
- Discuss code, behavior, risk, and tradeoffs rather than attacking people.
- Protect private information, credentials, security reports, and production details.
- Give clear attribution and accept constructive correction.
- Keep reviews focused and make space for contributors with different experience levels and communication styles.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, sexualized content, sustained disruption, impersonation, and retaliation are not accepted in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must use the private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Conflicts of interest should be disclosed so another suitable reviewer can participate when available.
